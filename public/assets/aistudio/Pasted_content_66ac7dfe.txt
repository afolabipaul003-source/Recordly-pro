
🚀 RECORDLY — MASTER DEVELOPMENT PLAN
Your records. Your business. Your control.
1. Our vision for Recordly
Recordly will be an expandable record-keeping and financial management platform designed for two main audiences:
Individuals and groups: People managing contributions, association dues, event collections, and group payments.
Small businesses: Business owners managing customers, sales, expenses, debts, and payments.
Over time, Recordly can expand into a broader management platform with additional tools.
Our central idea is simple:
One account. One dashboard. Multiple sections. Everything organized in one place.
We want to make Recordly useful for beginners while giving it enough flexibility to grow into a serious business.
2. 📱 Dashboard and navigation
We've decided to use a simple, mobile-style interface, rather than a complicated desktop-style sidebar on phones.
The design should be mobile-first, with clear icons, understandable labels, and easy navigation.
The main dashboard
When users open Recordly, they should see a quick overview of their records.
The dashboard should eventually display:
Total money recorded.
Total outstanding balances.
Number of active records.
Number of people or customers tracked.
Recent activities.
Upcoming payment deadlines.
Quick-action buttons.
Shortcuts to the different sections.
For example, users could tap a button to create a contribution record, record a sale, add a debt, or view a report.
Mobile navigation
We want a simple bottom navigation bar on phones, with access to the most important areas.
Because we plan to add several sections, we can put the most frequently used ones in the bottom bar and place the remaining sections under a clearly labeled More menu.
On larger screens, the layout can adapt to the available space.
First-time user experience
This is particularly important because someone has already told you they didn't understand how Recordly works.
We should introduce:
A short welcome screen.
A simple explanation of what Recordly does.
Guided first-time setup.
Clear descriptions of each section.
Helpful instructions when a section is empty.
Straightforward buttons such as Create Record, Add Customer, and Record Payment.
Our goal: A new user should understand how to get started without needing you to explain the entire app personally.

10. 🔐 Account security, privacy, and reliability
Bro, this is one area I don't want us to overlook.
If people are going to trust Recordly with financial records, security cannot be an afterthought.
Improvements we need
Reliable account creation and login.
Password recovery.
Secure logout.
Proper authorization for every user's records.
Protection against unauthorized data access.
Secure sharing links.
Appropriate privacy controls.
Safe payment editing and correction.
Prevention of accidental duplicate entries.
Reliable backups and a recovery plan.
Clear privacy and data-use policies.
Account deletion and appropriate data-deletion procedures.
Error handling that explains problems clearly.

Sharing controls
Users should be able to share the information needed for a particular purpose without exposing everything else.
For example, a member may need to see their own contribution progress, while a record administrator may need broader access to the group.
Those permissions must be enforced by the database and application—not merely hidden in the interface.
Accuracy
We should also test what happens when:
Someone enters an incorrect payment amount.
A payment is recorded twice.
A user loses internet connectivity.
A record is edited or deleted.
A user attempts to access another person's information.
A shared link is used by someone who isn't authorized to see private details.
We must resolve these issues before treating Recordly as ready for widespread use.

13. 💳 What about actual money transfers?
We've already made an important distinction in our discussions.
Recordly will initially record and organize payment information rather than process actual money transfers.
For example, someone could record that a customer paid ₦20,000 through a bank transfer.
Recordly would store the transaction details and update the customer's balance. It would not itself move the money.
In the future, we could investigate payment processing or payment collection integrations if they offer clear benefits.
That would require careful consideration of security, applicable Nigerian regulations, transaction fees, and suitable payment providers.
It is a potential future direction, not part of the current app's confirmed functionality.
